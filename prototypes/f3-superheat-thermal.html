MITIGATION Cyclic / edgefield realignment Borrowed strategy

Move injection toward the field periphery rather than continuous central infield injection, to avoid steam-column collapse — the core logic of Salak's SIRP (Acuña 2008; Kurniawan).

Applied here as an analogous strategy, not a conclusion Abdurachman 2023 itself reaches for Kamojang. Salak and Kamojang reservoir characteristics have not been confirmed equivalent — treat as an untested transfer until checked.
Audit trail — open items

  • Tier A's 2°C pilot-test drop has no stated control well/period in this panel — treat as associated with, not proven caused by, the C-3 injection until confounds (rainfall, production-rate changes) are excluded.
